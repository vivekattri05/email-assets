# BuildLikeNew.com × NewsBreak — Brand Sponsorship Playbook (Hinglish)

> Goal: Home-category manufacturers / brands ko cold email karke unke products ko
> **sponsored article / product feature / website ad space** ke through promote karna,
> aur isse ek steady side-income banana.
>
> Last updated: 15 Oct 2026

---

## 0. Pehle ek seedhi baat (Reality check)

Tumhare NewsBreak numbers achhe hain:

| Metric | Value (All time) |
|---|---|
| Impressions | 154M |
| Views | 13M (+5,467 aaj) |
| Followers | ~35.4K |
| Posts | ~6.5K |
| Likes / Comments / Shares | 157K / 45K / 127K |

Lekin pitch karne se pehle ye 4 cheezein clear honi chahiye, warna brands "no" bolenge ya baad mein problem hogi:

1. **NewsBreak views ≠ tumhari website ka traffic.** NewsBreak article ko apne app ke andar full dikhata hai. Reader zyada tar NewsBreak pe hi padhta hai, buildlikenew.com pe nahi aata. Isliye "website pe banner ad" bechne se pehle **Google Analytics (GA4) mein apni website ka asli monthly traffic** dekho. Brand ko jo number doge, wo sach hona chahiye.
2. **Tumhare views kahan se aa rahe hain?** Profile pe abhi top posts crime/murder news hain ("Three Found Dead…", "Woman Found Dead…", "Armed Robbers…"). Home-product brand ka sponsored article utne views nahi layega jitne crime stories laati hain. Pitch mein sirf **home-improvement / home-security articles** ke numbers dikhana — total 13M ko ek sponsored post ka "expected reach" bata dena misleading hoga.
3. **Brand safety (sabse bada risk).** Koi bhi lock / paint / flooring brand jab tumhara NewsBreak profile kholega aur upar "murder-suicide" dekhega, to uska marketing manager darr jayega. NewsBreak ne khud advertisers ko **Brand Suitability Controls** diye hain jisse wo specific publishers ko block kar sakte hain — matlab brands is cheez ko seriously lete hain.
   → Solution niche wale steps mein hai (Step 2).
4. **Ye income "thodi si" se start hogi.** Cold email mein reply rate kam hota hai. Pehle 1–2 mahine mein 1–3 paid deals aaye to bhi achha start hai. Isko ek system ki tarah chalao, lottery ki tarah nahi.

## 1. NewsBreak Policy — kya allowed hai, kya nahi (Research summary)

> ⚠️ Note: NewsBreak ka Community Standards page main seedha open nahi kar paaya (is environment mein site blocked thi), isliye ye summary unke official feed spec, help center, terms aur search results se bani hai. **Step 1 mein tum khud final confirm karoge.**

### ✅ Jo allowed lagta hai
- **Sponsored / branded / affiliate content allowed hai — lekin DISCLOSURE ke saath.** NewsBreak ki official RSS/MRSS Feed Specification bolti hai ki sponsored, branded, promotional aur affiliated content ka relationship clearly disclose hona chahiye. Feed mein ek disclosure field hota hai jiska text article ke **top pe** dikhta hai. Affiliate links ho to batana padega ki kisko fayda ho raha hai. Detail ke liye wo Community Standards ke **"Commercial and Promotional Content"** section ko refer karte hain.
- Article ke andar links — lekin **sirf editorial context mein** (Publisher FAQ). Matlab "Buy now" button spam nahi; natural, article ke flow mein link.

### ❌ Jo NOT allowed / risky hai
- **NewsBreak ke page / app pe ad space bechna — NO.** NewsBreak ke terms ke hisaab se NewsBreak apne platform pe advertising bechne aur uska revenue rakhne ka right rakhta hai. Tum sirf **apna content** (sponsored article) aur **apni website** ka space bech sakte ho. Brand ko kabhi mat bolna "NewsBreak pe aapka banner lagwa denge."
- **Clickbait / hyperbolic headlines** — NewsBreak ki terms clickbait ko prohibit karti hain. Sponsored article ka title normal, honest rakho.
- **Undisclosed paid content** — paisa liya aur "Sponsored" nahi likha = policy violation + US FTC violation. Strike / reduced distribution / removal ka risk.
- **Duplicate / thin promotional content** — sirf product description copy-paste mat karo. Asli review / guide likho.
- NewsBreak help center ye bhi kehta hai ki **NewsBreak khud** guest posts / sponsored content requests accept nahi karta — ye rule NewsBreak ko pitch karne walon ke liye hai, tumhare apne feed ke disclosed content pe ban nahi hai. Phir bhi Step 1 mein written confirmation le lo.

### US law (tumhare readers aur brands US mein hain)
- **FTC (native advertising):** paid content ko clearly label karo — "Sponsored", "Paid advertisement" ya "Sponsored content". Label **headline ke upar/pehle** ho. Vague words jaise sirf "Promoted" ya "Presented by" kaafi nahi maane jaate.
- **Google SEO (website ke liye):** sponsored links pe `rel="sponsored"` lagao. Google ki **site reputation abuse** policy un sponsored pages ko target karti hai jo site ke topic se bahar hon (jaise home-improvement site pe payday loan review). Isliye **sirf home-related brands** lo — ye tumhari niche ke andar hai, safe hai.

## 2. Step-by-step Plan

### STEP 1 — Policy pakki karo (Week 1, Day 1–2)
- [ ] NewsBreak Publisher Platform login karo → Community Standards ka **"Commercial and Promotional Content"** section poora padho. Screenshot save karo.
- [ ] Apne RSS feed / CMS mein dekho ki **disclosure field** kaise bhejna hai (feed spec PDF: support.newsbreak.com). Developer / plugin se ek test sponsored-style post bhej ke dekho ki top pe disclosure dikh raha hai.
- [ ] NewsBreak publisher support ko ek short email karo:
  > "We are a Partner Publisher (buildlikenew.com). We plan to occasionally publish clearly-labeled sponsored product reviews (home improvement only) on our site, with the sponsorship disclosure in the feed's disclosure field. Can you confirm this is compliant with the Community Standards?"
  
  Written "yes" mil jaye to tum safe ho aur brands ko bhi confidently bata sakte ho.

### STEP 2 — Apna "brand-safe" ghar saaf karo (Week 1)
Ye step skip mat karna — isi se brands haan bolenge.
- [ ] **Home content ka data nikaalo:** Analytics → *Export CSV* + Manage Content se dekho kaun se home-improvement / home-security / DIY articles ne kitne views, likes, shares liye. Top 10 home articles ki list banao.
- [ ] Average views per home article nikaalo. **Yahi tumhara pitch number hai** (total 13M nahi).
- [ ] Website pe ek **"Home & DIY"** section / category page clear rakho jahan sirf home content ho. Brands ko yahi link bhejo.
- [ ] Soch lo: crime news isi brand (BuildLikeNew) ke naam se chalani hai ya nahi. Long-term mein home-brand ke saath crime content mix karna sponsorship ke liye nuksaan hai. Kam se kam pitch ke time profile ke top pe kuch strong home articles hone chahiye.
- [ ] GA4 se website ka last 30 days ka users / pageviews / US % note karo.

### STEP 3 — Offer (packages) banao (Week 1)
Simple 3 packages rakho. Shuruaati prices (USD) — research ke hisaab se sponsored blog posts aam taur pe ~$100 se $2,000 tak jaate hain, size aur audience pe depend. Tum low-mid se start karo, 3–5 deals ke baad badhao.

| Package | Kya milega | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Product Spotlight** | 1 honest review / "how-to" article (800–1,200 words) jo product ko naturally use kare. buildlikenew.com pe publish + NewsBreak pe syndicate, "Sponsored" disclosure ke saath. Brand ka 1 link `rel="sponsored"` ke saath (do-follow nahi). 30-day performance report. | **$150–$300** |
| **B. Roundup Feature** | "Best smart locks for 2026"-type roundup mein brand ka featured slot (#1 position + photos). | **$100–$200** per slot |
| **C. Website Ad Space** | Home & DIY section pe sidebar / in-article banner, monthly. **Sirf tab bechna jab GA4 traffic decent ho** (warna price CPM pe rakho). | GA4 data ke baad decide |

Bonus options: product free mein bhejo to review karenge (product-for-post) — pehle 2–3 case studies banane ke liye ye bhi theek hai.

Rules jo har deal mein likhoge:
- Review honest hoga; brand ko final draft dikhayenge, par editorial control tumhara.
- Views ki **guarantee nahi** — sirf report milegi (NewsBreak algorithm tumhare control mein nahi).
- 50% advance, 50% publish ke baad (ya 100% advance chhote deals pe).

### STEP 4 — 1-page Media Kit banao (Week 1–2)
Canva / Google Docs mein 1 PDF:
1. BuildLikeNew kya hai (1 line) + logo
2. Audience: US, home owners, DIY / home security interest
3. Numbers: 35K NewsBreak followers, verified Partner Publisher badge, **home articles ka average reach**, website GA4 numbers
4. Top 3 home articles ke screenshots (views ke saath)
5. 3 packages + prices
6. Contact

### STEP 5 — Lead list banao (Week 2) — "kisko email karna hai"
**Bade brands (Home Depot, Moen, Ring) ko mat target karo** — wo agencies se kaam karte hain, reply nahi aayega. Target karo **mid-size / naye / DTC home brands** jo awareness chahte hain:

Niche-wise categories (tumhare content se match):
- Smart locks, video doorbells, security cameras, safes (home security — tumhari core niche)
- Power tools, hand tools, ladders, organizers
- Flooring, tiles, paint, wallpaper, sealants
- Water filters, water heaters, plumbing fixtures
- HVAC, air purifiers, dehumidifiers, smart thermostats
- Roofing, gutters, outdoor / patio, garage doors
- Cleaning gadgets, robot vacuums, pest control

Lead kahan se milenge:
1. **Amazon** → "Tools & Home Improvement" / "Smart Home" mein woh brands jinke 100–3,000 reviews hain (chhote, growth-mode wale). Brand store → unki website → contact.
2. **Trade show exhibitor lists** — KBIS (Kitchen & Bath Industry Show), IBS (NAHB International Builders' Show), National Hardware Show. Exhibitor list = ready-made manufacturer list.
3. **ThomasNet** — US manufacturers directory.
4. **Kickstarter / Indiegogo** — home gadgets ke naye launches (inko press chahiye hota hai).
5. **Competitors ke sponsors** — dusre home-improvement blogs / YouTube channels pe "Sponsored by" wale brands. Ye already paisa kharch karte hain!
6. **Shopify DTC home brands** — Google: `"home improvement" site:myshopify.com` ya Instagram ads library.

Contact kisko karna hai (priority order):
`Influencer / Partnerships Manager` → `PR / Communications Manager` → `Marketing Manager` → `Founder` (chhote brand mein).
Tools: LinkedIn, Hunter.io, Apollo.io (free tiers), brand ki "Press" / "Media" page.

Tracker: `lead-tracker-template.csv` file use karo. **Pehla target: 150 quality leads**, 500 random nahi.

### STEP 6 — Email setup (Week 2) — spam mein na jaye
- [ ] Main domain (buildlikenew.com) se bulk cold email **mat bhejo** — ek secondary domain lo, jaise `buildlikenewmedia.com` / `getbuildlikenew.com`, aur usse Google Workspace inbox (e.g. `partnerships@…`).
- [ ] **SPF, DKIM, DMARC** set karo, aur visible From domain inse aligned ho (Gmail/Yahoo ki requirement).
- [ ] Inbox ko 2–3 hafte warm-up karo (pehle normal mails, phir dheere dheere badhao).
- [ ] Shuru mein **20–30 emails/day max**, har email personalized.
- [ ] Spam complaint rate 0.1% se neeche rakho; 0.3% se upar gaya to deliverability toot jaayegi. Google Postmaster Tools connect karo.

### STEP 7 — Legal compliance (CAN-SPAM) — har email mein
US ka CAN-SPAM Act **B2B emails pe bhi lagta hai**. Har violating email pe ~**$53,088** tak penalty ho sakti hai. To:
- [ ] Har email ke footer mein **valid physical postal address** (virtual office / PO box chalega).
- [ ] Clear opt-out line ("Reply 'no thanks' and I won't email again" + unsubscribe link).
- [ ] Opt-out **10 business days ke andar** honour karo (better: same day) aur un logon ko dobara kabhi mat bhejo.
- [ ] Subject line misleading na ho (fake "Re:" / "Order issue" nahi).
- [ ] From name sahi ho (tumhara asli naam + BuildLikeNew).
- [ ] Phase 1 mein sirf **US brands**. EU (GDPR) aur Canada (CASL) ke rules zyada strict hain — unhe baad mein, samajh ke.

### STEP 8 — Outreach sequence (Week 3 se)
`email-templates.md` mein ready templates hain. Flow:

| Day | Email |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Email 1 — personalized intro + 1 specific idea for their product |
| Day 4 | Email 2 — short follow-up + top home article ka proof |
| Day 9 | Email 3 — "break-up" email / free review offer |

Personalization ka rule: har email mein **unke ek specific product ka naam** aur **ek article idea** jo tum likhoge. Generic "We have 13M views!" spam lagta hai.

### STEP 9 — Deal close + delivery (jab reply aaye)
1. 15-min call ya email pe brief lo: product, key points, links, deadline.
2. Simple 1-page agreement (Google Doc): scope, price, disclosure clause ("content will be labeled Sponsored"), no guaranteed views, payment terms, 1 revision.
3. Invoice — PayPal / Wise / Payoneer.
4. Article likho → brand ko draft → publish on website (Sponsored label top pe + `rel="sponsored"`) → NewsBreak feed mein disclosure field ke saath.
5. 7 din aur 30 din pe report bhejo (views, likes, shares, clicks — UTM link use karo taaki brand ko clicks dikhein).
6. Report ke saath next deal / monthly package offer karo. **Repeat clients hi asli income hain.**

### STEP 10 — Track + Scale (Month 2–3)
Weekly track karo:
- Emails sent → open → reply rate → deals → revenue
- Kaun si category zyada reply karti hai (e.g. security brands vs tools) → us pe double down.
- 3–5 deals ke baad: prices 30–50% badhao, case studies media kit mein daalo.
- Saath mein **affiliate programs** (Amazon Associates, brands ke Impact / ShareASale programs) bhi jodo — har review article se extra commission (disclosure ke saath).

## 4. Galtiyan jo nahi karni (Don'ts)
- ❌ "Sponsored" label chhupana / bhoolna
- ❌ NewsBreak pe ad space bechne ka promise
- ❌ 13M views ko ek post ka reach batana / views guarantee karna
- ❌ Crime content ke saath brand ka article place karna
- ❌ Main domain se bulk email, ya bina address / unsubscribe ke email
- ❌ Off-topic sponsors (casino, loans, crypto) — Google + brand image dono kharab
- ❌ Clickbait sponsored headlines

## 5. Tax / payments note
Foreign brands se aane wali income ka GST / income-tax treatment (export of services, LUT, etc.) apne CA se confirm kar lena.
