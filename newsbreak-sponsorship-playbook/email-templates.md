# Cold Email Templates (English — brands US mein hain)

> `{{ }}` wali cheezein har email mein badalni hain. Har email personalized ho.
> Footer har email mein zaroori hai (CAN-SPAM).

---

## Email 1 — Intro (Day 1)

**Subject options:**
- `{{Product}} feature idea for BuildLikeNew readers`
- `Quick idea: {{Product}} x home security guide`
- `{{Brand}} on BuildLikeNew?`

```
Hi {{FirstName}},

I run BuildLikeNew.com, a home improvement and home security publication. We're a
verified Partner Publisher on NewsBreak with 35K+ followers, mostly US homeowners.

I came across {{Product}} and think it'd fit well in a piece we're planning:
"{{ArticleIdea — e.g. 5 Ways to Secure a Back Door Without Calling a Locksmith}}".

Our home articles typically reach {{AvgHomeViews}} readers on NewsBreak, plus our
site. I'd write an honest, hands-on feature, clearly labeled as sponsored, and
send you a performance report after 7 and 30 days.

Open to a quick look at our 1-page media kit?

Thanks,
{{YourName}}
BuildLikeNew.com

—
{{Physical postal address}}
Not the right person or not interested? Reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.
```

## Email 2 — Follow-up (Day 4)

**Subject:** `Re: {{previous subject}}` *(sirf same thread mein reply ho to "Re:" use karo)*

```
Hi {{FirstName}},

Quick follow-up. Here's one of our recent home pieces for context:
{{Link to top home article}} — {{views}} views and {{shares}} shares on NewsBreak.

A {{Product}} feature would follow the same format. Happy to share the media kit
and rates if useful.

{{YourName}}

—
{{Physical postal address}} · Reply "no thanks" to opt out.
```

## Email 3 — Break-up / low-risk offer (Day 9)

**Subject:** `Close the loop?`

```
Hi {{FirstName}},

I'll stop here so I don't crowd your inbox. One last option: if you'd like to
test the fit, send us a {{Product}} unit and we'll consider it for an honest
review in our upcoming {{category}} roundup. No cost, no obligation either way.

If someone else handles partnerships, a name would be much appreciated.

Thanks,
{{YourName}}

—
{{Physical postal address}} · Reply "no thanks" to opt out.
```

## Reply — jab brand rates puchhe

```
Thanks {{FirstName}}! Media kit attached. Quick summary:

• Product Spotlight — dedicated hands-on article on BuildLikeNew.com + NewsBreak,
  labeled Sponsored, 30-day report: ${{price}}
• Roundup Feature — featured slot in "{{Best X for 2026}}": ${{price}}

We don't guarantee views (NewsBreak distribution is algorithmic), but we share
full stats plus click data via a tracked link. Typical turnaround is 7–10 days
after we receive the brief/product.

Would {{Product}} or {{Product2}} be the better fit to start?

{{YourName}}
```

## Personalization checklist (har email se pehle 2 minute)
- [ ] Unka 1 specific product naam
- [ ] 1 article idea jo tumhare readers ki problem solve kare
- [ ] Sahi person (partnerships / PR / marketing)
- [ ] Sirf home-category ka proof link (crime article nahi)
- [ ] Footer: address + opt-out
